# 📄 Admin CV & Category Management Guide

Welcome to the new CV and Category management system! This guide will help you manage your resume content dynamically.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  Log in to your admin panel.
2.  Navigate to the **CV & Categories** section in the sidebar.

## 📂 Managing Sections (Categories)

Sections represent the main blocks of your resume (e.g., Work Experience, Education, Skills).

### Adding a New Section
1.  Click the **Add Section** button.
2.  Enter the **Name** of the section.
3.  The **Slug** will be auto-generated (you can customize it).
4.  Set a **Display Order** (lower numbers appear first).
5.  Click **Save Section**.

### Editing/Deleting Sections
- Use the **Edit (pencil)** icon to update a section's name or order.
- Use the **Delete (trash)** icon to remove a section. **Note: Deleting a section will also delete all items within it.**

## 📝 Managing CV Items

Items are the individual entries within each section (e.g., specific jobs, schools, or skills).

### Adding a CV Item
1.  Click the **Add CV Item** button.
2.  Select the **Section** this item belongs to.
3.  Enter the **Title** (e.g., "Senior Developer").
4.  Enter the **Subtitle** (e.g., "Company Name" or "University").
5.  Specify the **Date Range** (e.g., "Jan 2020 - Present").
6.  Add a **Location** (e.g., "Bhubaneswar, India").
7.  Provide a **Description** of your roles or achievements.
8.  Set the **Display Order** within that section.
9.  Click **Save Item**.

## 🕒 Version Control & Rollbacks

Mistakes happen! Our system tracks all changes to your CV content.

- The **Version Control** table at the bottom shows recent updates.
- If you made a mistake during an update, click the **Rollback** button to restore the previous version of that item or section.

## 👤 Personal Information & CV Upload

Update your core details (Name, Email, Social Links, and Professional Summary) in the **Personal Information** card.

- **About Me / Professional Summary**: This field controls the multi-paragraph text in your "About" section. Use new lines (Enter key) to start new paragraphs.
- **Profile Photo / About Photo**: Upload a high-quality square image to be used as your profile photo throughout the site.

### 📄 CV PDF Upload
Instead of manual item entry for every section, you can now upload your professional CV as a PDF file:
1.  Go to the **Personal Information** section.
2.  Find the **CV PDF Upload** field.
3.  Choose your PDF file from your computer.
4.  Click **Save Changes**.
5.  Your portfolio will automatically link the "Download CV" button to this new file.

## 📂 Managing Categories

You can dynamically manage your Skill and Project categories from the **Categories** section in the admin sidebar.
- **Skill Categories**: Add categories like "Frontend", "Backend", or "Tools".
- **Project Categories**: Add categories like "Web Apps", "Mobile Apps", or "UI/UX Design".
- **Ordering**: Use the "Display Order" field to control which categories appear first (lower numbers = higher priority).
